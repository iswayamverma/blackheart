Our Story

Born
From Darkness

Black Heart was forged in the underground music scene of Delhi, where six musicians found a shared language in the space between rock and raw emotion. What began as late-night rehearsals in dimly lit studios grew into a sound that defies easy categorization.

The band blends contemporary rock, alt-metal, and cinematic soundscapes — creating music that doesn't just entertain, but resonates. Each performance is an experience, each track a statement.

Founded by Tarang Verma, with lead vocals by Shivam Rishant, the band has become a defining voice of the Delhi rock scene, performing at major venues and festivals across central India.
